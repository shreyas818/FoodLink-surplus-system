How FoodLink Works

Our real-time coordination dashboard ensures fresh surplus food is picked up and distributed safely within hours.

Step 1

List Surplus Food

Donors like hotels, caterers, and supermarkets upload their surplus food details, packaging type, shelf-life, and location. Our automated system estimates safety margins and formats the pickup details.

List Food
Step 2

Claim & Transport

Local logistics volunteers receive push notifications for nearby listings. Integrated route optimization algorithms guide them to the donor for pickup and navigate them to the destination NGO.

Claim Route
Step 3

Verified Distribution

Registered NGOs confirm safe arrival, unpack, and serve the meals immediately to families in need. Weight, temperature, and delivery confirmation codes verify the closed-loop logistics chain.
