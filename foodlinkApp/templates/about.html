The Cause We Support

The Paradox of Surplus & Scarcity

Every day, massive quantities of perfectly fresh, nutritious food are discarded by restaurants, wedding events, and caterers due to overproduction. At the exact same time, millions of families struggle to secure their next meal.

FoodLink was founded to dissolve this imbalance. We build the digital and physical pipelines required to capture surplus food at the source and deliver it securely to distribution agencies within its safe shelf-life.
